About us

We are a small company located in London, England.
We started out as just three guys,
wanting to make all TCGs available to everyone.

Our founder, John Smith, was fed up with not being able
to find stock of any TCG packs, and so he put together
a team to finally defeat scalpers once and for all.

Our mission here at Table Flip TCG is to make sure you
can get products of your favourite TCGs
without having to pay ridiculous prices.
Whether you are a beginner or master, player or collector,
We invite you to check out our store!

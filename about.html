About us

At Table-Flip TCG, our main goal is to bring Trading Card Games to players and collectors, both experienced and new.
We pride ourselves on our philosphy of never raising our prices above RRP, no matter what the market prices say.


Our Product Range

We specialise in a large array of different TCGs, including:

  • Pokémon
  • Magic: The Gathering
  • Lorcana
  • Cyberpunk


Beyond trading cards, we also specialise in a variety of accessories, including:

  • Sleeves
  • Binders
  • Dice and Counters
  • Deck Boxes



Our Commitment to our Customers

Here at Table-Flip TCG, customer satisfaction is our number one goal. We strive to provide a seemless experience, but
